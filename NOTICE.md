# Source and asset attribution

This repository presents the model and training approach developed in my MTP on efficient cascaded masked image modeling, with additional local evaluation. GILoRaT, related work I coauthored and used in the MTP training approach, is credited briefly in the README.

The code presented here was recreated from retained thesis sources and diagrams because the original training implementation and checkpoints were unavailable. This provenance is documented separately from the MTP method and its additional measurements.

Public datasets, pretrained components and dependencies retain their upstream terms. The pinned Nouns dataset, aMUSEd components, independent CLIP model and Inception evaluator are referenced by revision and SHA256 under `code/results/`. Their full weight/data files are not redistributed in this Git repository. No blanket license is assigned to third-party material.
