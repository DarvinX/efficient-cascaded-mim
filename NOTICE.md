# Source and asset attribution

This is a reconstruction and additional evaluation accompanying Tamal Hansda's MTP on efficient cascaded masked image modeling. It is not the missing original training implementation. The related GILoRaT work is credited in the README with its preprint DOI and author repository; the generative adaptation has separately documented settings and evidence.

Public datasets, pretrained components and dependencies retain their upstream terms. The pinned Nouns dataset, aMUSEd components, independent CLIP model and Inception evaluator are referenced by revision and SHA256 under `code/results/`. Their full weight/data files are not redistributed in this Git repository. No blanket license is assigned to third-party material.
